<h2>The talk</h2>
<p>
  I gave a talk on maximal and concentration inequalities for empirical measures built from mixing,
  dependent data, rather than i.i.d. samples. Empirical process theory gives sharp control of how an
  empirical measure fluctuates around its target under independence; the talk covers how much of that
  control survives once the independence assumption is dropped in favor of mixing conditions, and what
  the resulting inequalities are useful for in practice.
</p>

<h2>The challenge</h2>
<p>
  Participants write a trading bot in Python that competes against Akuna's own models and other
  entrants on a simulated exchange, over the course of a week. It's a practical test of the same
  market-making problem I work on professionally: quoting, managing inventory, and reacting to order
  flow, but under a fixed, competitive ruleset rather than production constraints.
</p>
